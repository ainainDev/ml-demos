SMOTE — Synthetic Sample Interpolator

Day 133 · Imbalanced Data. SMOTE fixes the accuracy trap without copying anyone: it builds brand-new minority points by interpolating between a minority point and one of its 5 nearest minority neighbours: X_new = X + λ(X_neighbour − X).

Each click picks random minority points, random neighbours and random λ — no point is ever copied.
SMOTE recipe (k = 5 neighbours):
1. Pick a minority point X.
2. Find its 5 nearest minority neighbours.
3. Choose one neighbour X_neighbour at random.
4. Choose λ in [0, 1] at random.
5. X_new = X + λ(X_neighbour − X)
X_new lands on the segment between X and its neighbour — a genuinely new point, not a duplicate.
