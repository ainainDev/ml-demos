Instance-Based vs Model-Based Learning

Day 6 · SAME dataset (IQ vs CGPA, blue = placed, red = not placed). Click on a plot to ask "will this student be placed?". Watch how the two learners answer differently.

Add-mode:

🧠 Instance-based — memorizes

K-nearest neighbour (k=1): finds the stored point closest to your click and copies its label.

Click anywhere on the plot to classify a new student.

📐 Model-based — generalizes

Logistic regression: learns ONE decision line from the data; classifies by which side a point falls on.

Click anywhere on the plot to classify a new student.
💾 Storage note: the instance-based learner must keep all N training points (N = right now) to answer anything — memorization is expensive. The model-based learner throws the data away and keeps just the line's parameters (w₁, w₂, b — three numbers). That is generalization.
