Rule of thumb — skew ≈ 0: symmetric, mean ≈ median · skew > 0: right-skewed, mean > median (tail stretches right, e.g. salaries, fares) · skew < 0: left-skewed, mean < median.

Why EDA cares about skew
Right-skewed (positive)
Most values small, a few very large. Mean is misleadingly high — the median describes "typical" better. Think salaries or Titanic fares.
Left-skewed (negative)
Most values large, a few very small. The mean is dragged down.
Symmetric (zero)
Mean and median agree. Many models (e.g. linear regression) are happiest when features look roughly like this — skew is why we later learn log transforms.
