ROC Curve & AUC — sweep the threshold

This synthetic classifier assigns every item a score (probability of being positive). Each threshold turns scores into one confusion matrix — one (FPR, TPR) point. Sweeping the threshold from 1 down to 0 traces the ROC curve, and the area under it is the AUC.

Step 1 — the threshold splits the scores

Negatives (class 0) Positives (class 1) TP FP FN Threshold

Step 2 — trace the ROC curve

Press the button to sweep the threshold from 1 → 0 and watch the curve draw itself. The curve bows because lowering a high threshold first adds mostly true positives. The diagonal is a coin-flip classifier.

TPR = TP/(TP+FN) — benefit
–
FPR = FP/(FP+TN) — cost
–
AUC (area under curve)
–
Best threshold = argmax(TPR−FPR)
