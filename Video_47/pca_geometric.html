📐 PCA: Rotate the Axes, Keep the Spread — Day 47 (Part 1)

The cloud below is elongated — points vary a lot along one direction and little along the other. Drag the axis and watch the projected "shadows".


Projected variance: 0.00
fraction of total variance kept by this axis
What this shows: Each point drops a perpendicular "shadow" (small tick) onto the red axis — that shadow is the 1-D projection. The spread of the shadows is the projected variance. PCA = rotate the axis until that spread is as big as possible. That best axis is PC1 (about 34° here): it keeps most of the cloud's shape with just one number per point. Project along the wrong axis and all shadows pile on top of each other — information is lost.
