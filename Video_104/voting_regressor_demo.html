🤖 Voting Regressor: Averaging Models for Regression Day 104 — every base model predicts a number; the ensemble predicts their average

Idea: train three different regressors on the same dataset, ask each one for a prediction, and take the mean: ŷ = (1/n) · Σ ŷᵢ. Below, a noisy curved dataset is fit by a least-squares line, a k-NN smoother and a small regression tree. Toggle models on and off, tune their knobs, and watch the bold green ensemble average smooth out their individual mistakes — the live RMSE panel scores everyone against the true curve.
