🤖 The Kernel Trick: Geometric Intuition Day 95 — when no line can separate the classes, lift the data until one can

Idea: an SVM draws a straight dividing line. Below, class X (blue) sits in the middle with class O (amber) on both sides — no single cut works. Apply the bell mapping f(x) = e−x² and every point gains a second coordinate; the middle class rides up onto the bell while the side class stays on the tails, and one horizontal line separates them.

Panel 2's "3D view" is a side projection: the vertical axis becomes z = e−(x²+y²). Inner-circle points (small x²+y²) rise high; the outer ring (large x²+y²) stays near the floor — so a flat plane slices between them.
