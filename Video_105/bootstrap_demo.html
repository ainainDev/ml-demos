🎯 Bootstrap sampling: the engine of bagging

A tiny 1-D classification problem (true boundary at x = 5, a little noise). Each "stump" is trained on its own bootstrap sample — 24 rows drawn with replacement from the same data. Watch how the stumps disagree… and how the majority vote smooths them out.

Sampling is with replacement: each stump's sample misses some points and repeats others — that is where the disagreement comes from. Stumps retrain automatically when you change M.
