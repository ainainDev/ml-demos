T1(x) = ea + ea(x−a) and T2(x) = T1(x) + ea/2 · (x−a)2. Watch how the 2nd-order curve hugs ex near a and drifts as |x−a| grows — exactly how XGBoost approximates its objective near the previous stage's prediction.

From Taylor to XGBoost: leaf-weight calculator

w* = −∑gi / (∑hi + λ)
Mapping to the video:
· Regression (MSE): gi = pred − y, hi = 1 → w* = ∑ residuals / (n + λ)
· Classification (log loss): gi = p − y, hi = p(1−p) → w* = ∑ residuals / (∑ p(1−p) + λ)
The −∑gi term is the familiar sum of residuals — the Taylor expansion is why that formula exists.
