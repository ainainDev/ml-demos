🗳️ Voting Ensemble: the Math Playground Day 102 — drag the sliders and watch the majority vote beat (or lose to) the single model

Idea: give n independent classifiers, each correct with probability p, and let the majority decide. The ensemble is correct exactly when more than half of the models are correct — a binomial sum. When each model beats 50%, the vote amplifies accuracy; below 50%, it amplifies the error. Try the video's presets to see both.
