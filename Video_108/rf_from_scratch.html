What to notice: in Bagging mode the split lines pile on top of each other — every tree leans on the same best feature, so they make the same mistakes. In Random Forest mode the lines scatter (trees are forced to use different features at different splits) and disagreement rises — yet the soft majority-vote map stays smooth and confident. Decorrelated trees + voting = the whole trick.
