Most people test negative, so the positives are dominated by false positives from the huge healthy crowd — that's why a 1% disease with a 95%-sensitive test still gives only ~16% chance of actually being sick after a positive result. Bayes' theorem forces you to weigh the evidence against the base rate (the prior). This "flip the condition" trick is exactly what Naive Bayes does with words and features.
