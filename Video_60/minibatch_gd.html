🌀 Mini-batch gradient descent playground

The loss is an elongated bowl: L = b² + 10·m². Descent uses a fixed learning rate, but the gradient noise shrinks as the batch grows — that is what morphs the path. Batch size 1 = pure SGD (noisy); 50 rows = full batch GD (smooth).
