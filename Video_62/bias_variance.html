🎚️ Bias–variance playground

Noisy data follows a hidden curve. Fit polynomials of increasing degree: degree 1 is the stiff M1 (underfit, high bias), the middle degrees are M2 (balanced), and degree 15+ is the wiggly M3 (overfit, high variance). Watch the train–test gap open up as complexity grows.

Controls
3

40 train points (×), 40 test points (•). Error = mean squared error. Both errors low and close together = the sweet spot; gap between them = variance at work.

The fit
— fitted curve × train points • test points
Train vs test error across degrees

Train error (blue) keeps falling as degree rises — but test error (red) forms a U: the tradeoff, exactly as Total Error = Bias² + Variance + σ² predicts.
