Agglomerative Clustering — Linkage & Dendrogram Explorer

Day 70 · Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering. Bottom-up: every point starts as its own cluster, and the two closest clusters merge, over and over, until one cluster remains. Each merge is drawn as a tree — the dendrogram — and linkage is the rule that decides how far apart two clusters are. Drag the cut line: every vertical branch it crosses becomes one cluster.
