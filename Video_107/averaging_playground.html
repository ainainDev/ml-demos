🤖 Averaging playground

Each thin blue curve is one wiggly model fitted on a bootstrap sample of the same noisy dataset — like the trees inside a BaggingRegressor. The thick green curve is their average. Drag the slider: as more models join the average, the prediction stops jumping around.

The bagged prediction is the mean of the m individual predictions — no voting, just averaging. σ̂/√m is the theoretical spread if the models were independent; bootstrap models overlap a little, so the real gain is slightly smaller, but the shrinkage is real: watch it fall as you drag m from 1 → 150.
