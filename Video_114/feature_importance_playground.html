📊 Feature Importance Playground

A Random Forest scores every column by how much impurity it removes across all its splits. Scores are normalized to sum to 1 — so this is a pie chart, not a report card: knock one feature down and the others must rise. Try shuffling a feature (breaking its link to the target, like permutation_importance does) and watch its bar collapse.

Shuffle a feature to see what happens to its importance score.

🧮 How each bar is computed (Gini importance)

Node importance: NI(j) = (nj/n)·I(j) − (nleft/n)·I(left) − (nright/n)·I(right)
(weighted impurity before the split, minus weighted impurities of the two children)

Feature importance (one tree): sum NI over every node split on that feature, ÷ sum of NI over all split nodes → sums to 1.

Random Forest: each tree computes its own importances, the forest reports the average across trees.

In code: rf = RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100); rf.fit(X, y); then rf.feature_importances_. ⚠️ sklearn warns that impurity-based importance can flatter high-cardinality features — permutation_importance is the safer check there.
