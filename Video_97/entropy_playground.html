🤖 Entropy & Information Gain Playground Day 97 — how mixed is a node, and how much does a split clean it up?

Idea: a decision tree splits on the feature that removes the most uncertainty. Uncertainty is measured with entropy H(p) = −p·log₂p − (1−p)·log₂(1−p) (peaks at p = 0.5) or its cheaper cousin Gini impurity G(p) = 2p(1−p). Information gain is the drop in entropy caused by a split: IG = H(parent) − Σ (nᵥ/n)·H(childᵥ).
