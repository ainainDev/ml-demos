DBSCAN — Density Explorer

Video 132 · DBSCAN (density-based clustering). No centroids, no "k" upfront — a point joins a cluster if it sits in a dense region. Two knobs control everything: eps (the neighbourhood radius) and min\_samples (how many points must sit inside that radius). Dense regions become clusters; isolated points become noise.

⚔️ Why centroid-based clustering fails here

K-Means (k = 2) must draw a straight line through the middle and split both rings in half — it can never trace a ring around another ring. DBSCAN sees density, not centres.
