What's happening: ŷ = mean(y) + lr × (tree₁ + tree₂ + …), where treeₖ is fitted on the residuals of the previous step. Lower the learning rate and watch the error curve: the steps shrink, so more rounds are needed — but the fit gets smoother and overfits less.

The little trees are depth-2 regression trees (≤ 4 leaves), like the small trees in the lesson. Try learning rate 1.0 with 12 rounds vs 0.1 with 12 rounds: big steps learn fast but wiggle; small steps learn slowly but smoothly.
