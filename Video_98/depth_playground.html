The math (kept honest): Gini impurity of a node is G = 1 − (p₀² + p₁²). Every candidate axis-aligned split is scored by gain = G(parent) − (nL/n)·G(left) − (nR/n)·G(right); the tree takes the max-gain split (splitter="best"), stops a branch at max\_depth, when the node is pure, or when it holds fewer than min\_samples\_split = 2 points.
