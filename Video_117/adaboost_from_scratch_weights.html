AdaBoost from Scratch — live weight evolution

This demo runs a real 3-round AdaBoost in your browser: 10 training points, decision-stump search each round (like DecisionTreeClassifier(max_depth=1)), equal start weights of 1/10, error → α → weight update → normalize → weighted resample. Scrub the rounds and watch the hard points grow while the easy ones shrink. (Deterministic — same numbers every visit. An epsilon guard keeps α finite if a round ever has zero error.)
