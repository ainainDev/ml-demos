📊 Which plot for which pair?

The plot you choose depends on the data types of the two columns. Pick types for X and Y, toggle a third variable (hue), and watch the correct chart draw itself on synthetic restaurant data.

Add hue (a third categorical column) to any plot and bivariate analysis becomes multivariate — more variables in one chart. A heatmap of the correlation matrix and a pairplot (every numeric pair at once) are the multivariate workhorses for the next steps.
