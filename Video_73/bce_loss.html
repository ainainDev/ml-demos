Model 2 wins — it backed the true classes more strongly. Maximum likelihood, in action.

Why the log?

Multiplying thousands of tiny probabilities makes the product collapse to zero on a computer (underflow). log turns the product into a sum and never changes the winner. Minimizing the negative of that sum is exactly what gradient descent does next.
