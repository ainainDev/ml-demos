With only a few tosses the empirical gap jumps around — small samples are noisy. As N grows, the gap between P(A∩B) and P(A)·P(B) collapses toward zero: the two coins really are independent. Contrast this with mutually exclusive events (Video 84, Part 3), where P(A∩B) is stuck at 0 — independence and mutual exclusivity are opposites, not synonyms.
